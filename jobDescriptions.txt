accountant job description

We are seeking a detail-oriented Accountant to join our team on a full-time basis. In this role, you will be responsible for managing financial records, ensuring regulatory compliance, and supporting strategic decision-making through accurate and timely reporting. Key responsibilities include maintaining and reconciling general ledger accounts, preparing monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements, processing accounts payable and receivable, managing payroll, performing bank reconciliations, and conducting cash flow analysis. You will also prepare tax returns, ensure compliance with all applicable regulations, assist with budgeting and forecasting, conduct internal audits, implement financial controls, and provide support to external auditors during year-end audits.

The ideal candidate holds a Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field, with CPA or CMA certification preferred. We require 2–5 years of relevant accounting experience, including hands-on work with accounting software such as QuickBooks, SAP, or ERP systems, as well as proficiency in Microsoft Excel. Strong knowledge of GAAP or IFRS is essential, along with excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, a high degree of attention to detail and accuracy, and strong written and verbal communication abilities. The successful candidate must be capable of working effectively under pressure and meeting tight deadline
